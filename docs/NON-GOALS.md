# NON-GOALS (M0)

- No solver integration (preflop or postflop).
- No multiway solver approximations.
- No reinforcement learning or self-play training loops.
- No solver-backed bots (bots are heuristic-only).
- No dependence on any one engine’s internal evaluator for **decision-making** logic (evaluator is for showdown/sanity, not strategy).
- No real-money integrations or third-party poker clients.
- No multi-table/multi-user networking; single local user only.
